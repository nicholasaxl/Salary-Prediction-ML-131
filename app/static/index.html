Estimates come from a 10th–90th percentile LightGBM model. In cross-validation that interval covered about 77.5% of held-out salaries (nominal 80%), and the median interval was about $115k wide: features like title, level and location explain only around 40% of salary variance. The data is a self-reported survey sample, so effects shown are associations, not causes.
